      Frequently Asked Questions

      Clear, transparent answers to how we work, onboarding timelines, and campaign operations.

      How quickly can GoAdsLive launch our new ad campaigns?
      Our standard onboarding takes 3 to 5 business days. During this sprint, we complete your tracking audits (Pixel, CAPI, GA4), produce initial AI & UGC creative hooks, set up campaign architecture, and obtain your sign-off before going live.
      What minimum ad spend budget is required to work together?
      We work with fast-growing brands spending anywhere from ₹50,000/month up to ₹50+ Lakhs/month. Our growth models are flexible and designed to generate enough immediate ROAS to self-fund higher ad spend tiers.
      Are we locked into long-term agency contracts?
      No. We believe client retention must be earned through consistent ROI every single month. We operate on rolling 30-day performance agreements with zero restrictive long-term lock-ins.
      How do you handle ad creatives and video content?
      Our in-house creative studio handles the entire lifecycle: copywriting, direct-response script writing, creator sourcing for UGC, AI voiceover and video variations, and graphic design. You receive a steady pipeline of fresh creatives weekly to eliminate ad fatigue.
      How do we track performance and communication?
      You get a 24/7 real-time Google Looker performance dashboard displaying live ROAS, CPA, MER, and revenue numbers. Additionally, we set up a dedicated private WhatsApp/Slack channel and conduct weekly strategic review calls.
